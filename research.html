Research

Understanding the microbiome in health and disease

Our aim is to develop novel bioinformatic and computational methods to better understand the role of the microbiome in disease, within an inclusive lab culture. The work rests on three connected pillars.

01

Collaborative health projects

We work with clinicians and scientists to better understand human and microbial interactions in health and disease, bringing advanced bioinformatic methods to their studies.

Crohn diseaseLung cancerChild oral healthAging and frailty
02

Application and resource development

We create and validate bioinformatic approaches that improve the biological insight we can draw from microbiome data, and release them as open software.

Functional predictionLow biomass samplesMetagenomics

03

Integrated Microbiome Resource

A sequencing and bioinformatics service housed within the lab. The IMR offers current sequencing technologies and deep expertise in microbiome sequencing and analysis to scientists around the world.
