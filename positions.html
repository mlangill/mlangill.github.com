Join us

Research opportunities

Interested in the microbiome? Want to join an interdisciplinary group of researchers while gaining skills in genomics and bioinformatics? We would like to hear from you.

The Langille Lab welcomes trainees from many backgrounds and disciplines and values the diversity of all applicants. Our members come to microbiome research from biology, medicine, computer science and beyond, and learn to work with large sequencing datasets on real health questions.

How to apply

Please complete our short application form. It helps us keep track of interested applicants and match them with projects and funding as they become available.
